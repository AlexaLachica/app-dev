# My Favorite Anime Series: Sakamoto Days
 
## About the Anime
The story follows Taro Sakamoto, who was once the most feared and legendary hitman in the underworld. After falling in love with a convenience store clerk, he abruptly retired from assassinations, got married, had a child, and gained a lot of weight living a peaceful life.
 
### Why I like the show
- There are cool fight scenes and full of unexpected moments
- The characters are very fun and interesting with how different their personalities are and how they interact with each other.
- I like how the anime balances funny moments and exciting battles
